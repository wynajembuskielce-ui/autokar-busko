## 6. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the following rights:

1. **Right of access** (Art. 15) — you may obtain a copy of your data,
2. **Right to rectification** (Art. 16) — you may request correction of your data,
3. **Right to erasure / "to be forgotten"** (Art. 17) — in specific situations,
4. **Right to restrict processing** (Art. 18),
5. **Right to data portability** (Art. 20) — in a machine-readable format,
6. **Right to object** (Art. 21) — particularly to marketing,
7. **Right to withdraw consent** at any time (Art. 7(3)) — withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal,
8. **Right to lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority — **President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO)**, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, [www.uodo.gov.pl](https://www.uodo.gov.pl). You may also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

To exercise these rights, write to **biuro@muszkieter.pl** with subject "GDPR" — we will respond within 30 days.
